# IONOS pricing and renewal breakdown

IONOS runs US data centers in Newark (NJ), Las Vegas (NV), and Lenexa (KS).

Its shared plans (`IONOS Plus` at `$1.00/mo`) jump sharply to `$14.00/mo` in year 2 (`+1,300%`), bringing the 36-month effective cost to `$9.67/mo`. By contrast, IONOS VPS Linux XS is a flat `$2.00/mo` with no renewal increase.

| IONOS Web Hosting Plus | `$1.00/mo` | 12 mo | `$14.00/mo` | `$348.00` | `$9.67/mo` |
