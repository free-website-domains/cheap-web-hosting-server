# Hostinger pricing and renewal breakdown

Hostinger operates US data centers in Phoenix (AZ), Boston (MA), and Asheville (NC), using hPanel on shared hosting and root SSH on VPS plans.

The catch is term length: the headline `$2.99/mo` (Premium) and `$3.99/mo` (Business) rates require paying 48 months upfront. Shorter 12-month terms cost more per month, and plans renew at `$7.99/mo` to `$8.99/mo`.

| Plan | Promo Rate | Promo Term | Renewal Rate | 36-Mo Effective |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hostinger Premium | `$2.99/mo` | 48 mo | `$7.99/mo` | `$2.99/mo` (if prepaid) |
| Hostinger Business | `$3.99/mo` | 48 mo | `$8.99/mo` | `$3.99/mo` (if prepaid) |
| Hostinger KVM 1 VPS | `$4.99/mo` | 24 mo | `$9.99/mo` | `$6.66/mo` |
